# Challenge

**Big Idea:** Scalability

**Essential Question:** How do I know when my current database design will break, and what do I do before it does?

**Challenge:** Design a database stress-testing and monitoring system that detects bottlenecks before they cause failures in production — and use each bottleneck as a lens into the broader system design patterns (caching, load balancing, queues) that solve it at scale.
