# Horizontal vs vertical scaling — when do you hit the limits of a bigger server?

**Status:** answered

Answered by [[cassandra-distributed-architecture]] — a concrete example of scaling
**out** (adding equal nodes to a cluster/ring) instead of scaling **up** a single
server. See also [[discord-database-evolution]] for what hitting the limit looks like,
and [[scaling-up-vs-out]] for DDIA's canonical framing (shared-nothing, elastic,
single-node-until-forced).
